2. CER — Average Velocity vs. Average Speed

Question: Under what condition will average velocity equal average speed, and when will they differ?

  • Claim: State the exact motion condition required for average velocity magnitude to equal average speed.
  • Evidence: Compare trips with no turnarounds (Trip 1) against turnaround trips (Trip 5/6).
  • Reasoning: Relate the definitions vavg = Δx / Δt and Speedavg = d / Δt to the path direction.
3. CER — Why Average Velocity is NOT (v₁ + v₂) / 2

Question: Why is the overall average velocity almost never equal to the simple arithmetic average of the two segment speeds?

  • Claim: Explain why average velocity is a time-weighted average rather than an unweighted average.
  • Reasoning: The car spends more time at the slower speed than at the faster speed. Therefore, the slower speed pulls the true average velocity closer to itself!

Question 4: Segment Velocities & Overall Average Velocity

Equation: v = Δx / Δt

  • Segment 1 (Outbound): Moves in positive direction (Δx₁ > 0), so velocity v₁ > 0.
  • Segment 2 (Inbound): Moves in negative direction (Δx₂ < 0), so velocity v₂ < 0.
  • Total Trip: Since the walker returns to the exact starting location, xf = x0 = 0 m, making Δx = 0 m and vavg = 0 m/s!
Question 5: Arithmetic Average vs. Average Velocity

Question: Is the arithmetic average (v₁ + v₂) / 2 the same as the average velocity?

Explanation: No. The arithmetic average simply adds the numbers and divides by 2. It does not account for the fact that the walker spent different amounts of time moving in each direction. Average velocity is strictly defined as Total Displacement / Total Time.

Question 6: Average Speed vs. Average Velocity

Question: Is average speed the same as average velocity? Explain using the difference between total distance and displacement.

Explanation: No. Displacement is a vector that accounts for direction (and canceled to 0 m), whereas distance is a scalar representing the total ground covered (20 m). Thus, average speed is positive (> 0 m/s) while average velocity is zero (0 m/s).
